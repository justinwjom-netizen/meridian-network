The cycle

Intent to settlement in seven moves.

1

Policy

You set limits in plain English; schema-validated before anything is stored.

2

Strategist proposes

Market state + policy → a proposed trade and the intelligence it needs.

3

Signal + Risk

Specialist agents are purchased; results released only after payment settles.

4

HSP mandate

A signed mandate with unique nonce + expiry authorises payment within budget.

5

Settle + execute

Payment settles on HSK Chain; the approved swap executes on HSKSwap.

6

Audit

Veritas verifies and signs the epoch PnL attestation.
